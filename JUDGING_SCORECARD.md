## Expert dimensions

- Security correctness: PASS for covered local controls and bounded property/fuzz/chaos suites; broad accessibility, production, and broader research coverage NOT PROVEN.
- Protocol correctness: PASS for tested MCP paths; upstream/DNS boundary limitations remain.
- Evaluation quality: reproducible 18-scenario × 3-repetition harness with protected, unprotected, and static paths; production study, independent baselines, and release-grade research bundle NOT PROVEN.
- Developer experience: local install and TypeScript/Python examples documented; setup timing needs clean-machine evidence.
- Product coherence: execution trust layer is coherent at the supported local boundary.
- Commercial feasibility: design-partner hypotheses documented; customer and pricing evidence NOT PROVEN.
- Trust and transparency: explicit status labels and superseded historical reports.

## Current score rule

No category receives 10/10 until the corresponding evidence gate is directly executed and independently reviewed. The overall product-transformation verdict remains `NOT READY` while mandatory P0, P1, P2, clean-rehearsal, audit, accessibility, or production-evidence gates remain unresolved.
