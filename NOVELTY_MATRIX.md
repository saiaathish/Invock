## Interpretation

The strongest defensible positioning is a hypothesis about composition and workflow: Invock focuses on a local MCP mediation boundary where protocol identity, canonicalized action facts, policy, attenuated authority, selected data lineage, and signed receipts can be evaluated together. This is a product/design differentiation hypothesis, not a finding of academic or patent novelty. Any external claim should cite the specific property and the specific executed evidence supporting it.
